**QuantaJS 3.0.** All packages now release together under one version, and the framework-free core gains official Vue (`@quantajs/vue`) and Svelte (`@quantajs/svelte`) bindings alongside React. 3.0 removes APIs that were deprecated or internal, types persistence end to end, and ships a smaller ES build. Migration guide: https://quantajs.com/docs/getting-started/migration
